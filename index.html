Professional Profile

I am a results-driven Business Analyst with experience in business analysis, project management, data analysis, process improvement, and strategic decision-making. I have a strong interest in understanding how businesses operate, identifying challenges and inefficiencies, and developing practical solutions that improve processes, support better decision-making, and create measurable business value.

My experience has given me the ability to work across different stages of the business and project lifecycle, from understanding business needs and gathering requirements to analysing processes, defining solutions, supporting implementation, and evaluating outcomes. I have successfully contributed to projects by working closely with stakeholders and cross-functional teams to understand their objectives, identify gaps, and translate business needs into clear and actionable requirements.

I take a structured approach to solving business problems. I use techniques such as requirements elicitation, stakeholder analysis, process mapping, gap analysis, root-cause analysis, and As-Is/To-Be analysis to understand current processes and identify opportunities for improvement. I am also comfortable documenting business requirements, developing user stories and acceptance criteria, and supporting communication between business and technical teams.

Data is an important part of my approach to Business Analysis. I use Excel, SQL, and Power BI to analyse information, identify trends, monitor performance, and transform data into meaningful insights. I enjoy using data visualisation and storytelling to present complex information in a clear and understandable way, enabling stakeholders to make informed business decisions.

I also have experience with Agile project management and SDLC, using tools such as Jira, Trello, Azure DevOps, Confluence, Lucidchart, and draw.io. These tools support my ability to manage requirements, document processes, track project activities, collaborate with teams, and communicate solutions effectively.

One of my key strengths is my ability to connect business needs, processes, data, people, and technology. I understand that a successful solution is not simply about introducing new technology; it must address the underlying business problem, meet stakeholder requirements, be practical to implement, and deliver the expected benefits.

Through the projects featured in this portfolio, I demonstrate my ability to analyse real-world business problems, assess current-state processes, develop future-state solutions, interpret data, manage requirements, and contribute to successful project outcomes.

I am continuously developing my Business Analysis and project management capabilities, with a focus on continuous improvement, strategic thinking, data-driven decision-making, and delivering solutions that create lasting value for organisations.

BrewConnect – Digital Brewery Management System

A business analysis and digital transformation project developed for A Brewing Group UK. The project examined operational challenges including manual processes, production delays, outdated systems, inventory management issues, reactive maintenance, and communication gaps between departments.

The project also developed a Business Requirements Document (BRD), financial analysis, risk assessment, implementation plan, and expected business benefits for the proposed BrewConnect solution.
